😊 Happiness

Happiness directly affects your XP and coin bonuses. A happy pet gives you more rewards!

📉 What Decreases Happiness

  • • Time: -1.5 happiness per hour passively
  • • Hunger: Extra penalty when hunger > 50%
  • • No interaction: -10 happiness if no interaction for 12+ hours

📈 How to Increase Happiness

  • • 🍖 Feed: Use food items with happiness bonus
  • • 🎾 Play: Use toys (+15 happiness by default)
  • • ❤️ Give Gifts: Use heart items (+15 happiness by default)
  • • 📚 Learn Together: Do reviews to gain small happiness boosts

Mood Levels

🤩 90%+ = Ecstatic
😊 70-89% = Happy
😌 50-69% = Content
😐 30-49% = Neutral
😢 10-29% = Sad
😭 0-9% = Miserable

⚡ Energy

Energy is required to play with your pet. It regenerates naturally over time.

📉 What Uses Energy

  • • 🎾 Playing with Toys: -15 energy per play session (default)
  • • 🎮 Playing Games: -10 to -20 energy per game (based on score tier)
  • • Minimum required: 10 energy to play

📈 How Energy Regenerates

  • • ⏰ Time: +2 energy per hour automatically (max 100)
  • • 💊 Medicine: Some items restore energy
  • • ❤️ Heart Items: Some heart items give energy bonus

💡 Tip

Energy regenerates slowly (+2/hour). From 0 to 100% takes about 50 hours. Plan your games wisely!

🍖 Fullness (Hunger)

Fullness shows how well-fed your pet is. Low fullness (high hunger) can damage your pet's health!

📉 What Decreases Fullness

  • • ⏰ Time: Hunger increases +2 per hour (fullness decreases)

📈 How to Feed Your Pet

  • • 🍖 Feed: Use food items from the shop
  • • ⏱️ Cooldown: 1 hour between feedings

⚠️ Danger Levels (Hunger)

  • 0-49% ✅ Healthy
  • 50-69% ⚠️ Getting hungry
  • 70-79% 🔶 -1 HP/30 min
  • 80-89% 🔴 -3 HP/30 min
  • 90-100% 💀 -5 HP/30 min

❤️ Health (HP)

CRITICAL! If HP reaches 0 and stays there for 24 hours, your pet will die permanently!

📉 What Damages HP

  • • Hunger 70-79%: -1 HP every 30 min
  • • Hunger 80-89%: -3 HP every 30 min
  • • Hunger 90-100%: -5 HP every 30 min
  • • Happiness ≤ 20%: -1 HP every 30 min
  • • Happiness ≤ 10%: -3 HP every 30 min

📈 How to Heal HP

  • • 💊 Medicine: Use healing items from the shop
  • • 🍖 Food: Some food items have HP bonus
  • • ⏰ Natural: +2 HP every 30 min when healthy

💀 Death Mechanic

  • • When HP reaches 0, a 24-hour timer starts
  • • You'll receive urgent notifications
  • • Use revival items or heal quickly!
  • • After 24 hours at HP=0, pet dies PERMANENTLY
